Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

1. Overview

School Quiz ("we", "our", or "the app") provides interactive learning quizzes, FLN foundational tests, and performance statistics for students in Classes 6 through 10. We take the privacy of students and educational users seriously.

2. Information Collection

Our app collects minimal information strictly necessary to provide educational functionality:

  • Student Profile: Name, class standard, preferred language, and avatar selected by the student.
  • Learning Data: Quiz scores, completed units, attendance streaks, and earned achievement badges stored locally and optionally synced via Firebase Firestore.
  • Authentication: When signing in with Google or email, account credentials are handled securely by Google Identity Services and Firebase Authentication.

3. Data Protection & Student Safety

We do not sell student data, show targeted third-party behavioral ads, or require unnecessary device permissions. Photo selection uses the zero-permission Android Photo Picker without accessing general device storage.

4. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact our support team.
